# Workspace Pane Manual Verification

- [ ] New workspace launch: select a configured project and confirm the workspace opens with only the configured shell.
- [ ] Split right: press `Ctrl+Shift+S` and confirm a shell pane opens to the right without launching Neovim.
- [ ] Split down: press `Ctrl+Shift+D` and confirm a shell pane opens below without launching Neovim.
- [ ] Zoom: press `Ctrl+Shift+Z` and confirm the active pane toggles between zoomed and tiled.
- [ ] Select: press `Ctrl+Shift+O`, choose a pane label, and confirm that pane receives focus.
- [ ] Navigate: use `Ctrl+Shift+h/j/k/l` and confirm focus moves between terminal panes.
- [ ] Resize: press `Ctrl+Shift+Alt+h/j/k/l`, then `1…9`, and confirm the boundary moves by 10%…90% of the active pane width or height.
- [ ] Close: press `Ctrl+Shift+W` and confirm the active pane closes only after confirmation.
- [ ] Snacks explorer root: press `<leader>e` or `<leader>fe` and confirm Snacks explorer opens at the project root.
- [ ] Snacks explorer cwd: press `<leader>E` or `<leader>fE` and confirm Snacks explorer opens at the current working directory.
- [ ] Neovim splits: confirm `Ctrl+h/j/k/l` and `Alt+h/j/k/l` remain available to smart-splits inside Neovim.
